# Merchant Practical Analytics & Decision Guide

This guide bridges technical metrics with everyday store decisions, explaining how merchants interpret dashboard figures and what concrete actions to take.

---

## 1. Core Revenue & Cash Questions

### Question 1: "How much money have I actually received vs. how much is still in transit?"
- **The Metric:** **Paid Sales** vs. **Total Booked Sales** (including Cash on Delivery).
- **The Meaning:** 
  - *Paid Sales:* Money digitally captured or cash physically collected. You can withdraw and spend this.
  - *Total Booked Sales:* Everything ordered. The gap between them is money currently out with couriers or pending delivery confirmation.
- **Actionable Decision:**
  - If the gap is wide (> 30% of sales sitting in uncollected COD), tighten courier delivery confirmations or incentivize digital checkout with free shipping.

### Question 2: "Is my revenue movement driven by order volume or basket size?"
- **The Metrics:** **Paid Orders** paired with **Average Order Value (AOV)**.
- **The Meaning:**
  - If Sales are up and Orders are flat, customers are buying higher-tier products or more items per order (AOV increased).
  - If Sales are flat and Orders are up, customers are buying cheaper items or discounts are eroding margins.
- **Actionable Decision:**
  - When AOV drops: Introduce product bundles (e.g. "Buy 2 Save 15%") or set a minimum order threshold for free shipping.

## 2. Operational Backlogs & Preventing Lost Money

### Question 3: "Why am I losing orders before they even ship?"
- **The Metric:** **Orders Needing Decision** and **Auto-Cancelled Orders**.
- **The Meaning:** Orders where the customer paid, but the store failed to accept within the required window.
- **Actionable Decision:**
  - Enable push/email notifications for new orders.
  - Check the "Orders Need Decision" queue first thing in the morning to prevent automated cancellation refunds.

### Question 4: "What products are about to cost me sales by running out?"
- **The Metric:** **Inventory Attention (Low Stock & Out of Stock)**.
- **The Meaning:** Tracked items with $\le 5$ available units remaining.
- **Actionable Decision:**
  - Reorder from suppliers immediately.
  - If replenishment will take weeks, mark the product as "Pre-order" or temporarily pause paid social ads driving traffic to that SKU.

---

## 3. Merchandising & Product Performance

### Question 5: "What are my bestsellers, and what is dead stock?"
- **The Metric:** **Top Products (by Paid Units)**.
- **The Meaning:** Identifies the core 20% of your catalog generating 80% of your sales.
- **Actionable Decision:**
  - Place bestsellers prominently on your storefront homepage.
  - Identify zero-sale products (dead stock) and run promotional clearance discounts to free up cash.

---

## 4. Summary: The Daily 5-Minute Dashboard Routine

```text
Step 1: Check "Orders Need Decision"  ──► Accept all pending customer orders.
Step 2: Check "Fulfillment Backlog"   ──► Generate shipping labels & dispatch packages.
Step 3: Check "Inventory Attention"    ──► Reorder items with Low Stock warnings.
Step 4: Check "Paid Sales & Orders"    ──► Review 30-day revenue rhythm and AOV.
Step 5: Check "Available Balance"     ──► Request payout to bank if working capital needed.
```
